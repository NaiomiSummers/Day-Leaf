DayLeaf

Health data privacy

Your entries and their sources

You supply journal entries and drafts, symptoms, medications, daily activities, questionnaire answers, worksheets, and optional safety-plan notes and contacts. You can also select an encrypted backup to restore. DayLeaf uses these records locally to show history, calculate screening totals and prepare reports. Journal records and drafts are encrypted in the browser vault. Someone using the unlocked journal can read them.

What leaves when you choose

A .dat backup is encrypted. PDF reports and printouts are readable. Preview the report, dates and optional sections before saving, printing or opening the device share sheet. A chosen app or recipient can receive the report. Calls and texts open the device handler only when you choose the link. Recording a support note or completing a screen sends no message. Opening an original-source link or the Apple Reminders guide contacts that website.

Connection to the host

On GitHub Pages, opening this app downloads its public files. The browser requests app resources and checks the current Terms on opening, returning to the app or reconnecting. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. These requests retrieve program files without including entries, screening answers or reports. The app keeps its versioned agreement receipt in this browser.

GitHub Pages data collection

Saving entries or completing a screen does not automatically send them to a clinician or the publisher. A hosting request can still identify the DayLeaf resource requested. The application asks for no permission to send health records when you agree to the Terms.

Managing copies and requesting help

To remove the browser copy, clear this app’s site data in the browser or installed profile that holds the records. Site-data controls can cover other apps on the same origin. Clearing storage also removes local agreement receipts. Manage downloads, printed copies and files shared with other people separately. The publisher has no remote control in this app to erase your browser records or files.

Feedback links open your mail program with an app name and subject. You choose what to send. For privacy questions, email NaiomiSummersDevelopment@gmail.com. Describe the question without including personal records or passwords.
